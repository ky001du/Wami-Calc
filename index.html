Plaza building costs

These 10 buildings (not Ancient Library or Demon Market) don't give individual bonuses — leveling them earns "Perks" toward Plaza Prestige, a nested reset: all 10 drop to 0, PlazaPrestigeCount +1, and you bank Perks currency (spent in the Plaza Perk shop, not modeled here). Each level burns permanent mana cap plus one Gathering resource, drawn the same amount from all 3 Void/Astral/Divine tracks at once — confirmed to escalate by tier, not the same resource for every building: Hell Well/Aqueduct/Arena/Commando Post cost Log, Magic Circle/Genetic Lab/Dwarven Workshop/Adventures Guild cost Ore, Trainings Dummy/Scout Camp/Ancient Library/Demon Market cost Gem. Most need Pet Stage 165+.

Formulas read from the game's code (three level tiers per building). Not modelled: the extra unlock requirements beyond stage (e.g. Arena needs Aqueduct and Hell Well at 5+). The wiki's Plaza page lists an individual effect per building (e.g. "Arena: +10% Battlefield unit damage") — the game's own code disagrees: none of the 10 Prestige buildings have a live bonus field or any downstream consumer of their level besides PerksGain, confirmed directly in PlazaTown's fields and PlazaPrestige.Update. Likely the wiki is describing an older version, from before Plaza was reworked into the Perks/Prestige system — flagging the conflict rather than picking a side silently.

Generator (Power/Mana)

All 15 bonus types share the same two global essence counters (confirmed from a live screenshot: Mana Refill and Mana Power read identical values because they use the identical formula on the identical essence pool — this isn't a coincidence, the game really does share one pool across every type). Charges (max 3 base, +1 every 6h, Soul Shop can add more/speed it up) aren't modeled as a timer here — enter your current Spent/Current Essence directly. Formula per type: bonus% = coef × 3^(const + rate × log_base(essence)), confirmed structurally from the game's own code (3^(…) growth curve, per-type coefficient table) and cross-checked against the wiki's published formula table, which matched exactly for 10 of 11 "simple" types (Gold's published current-coefficient looked like a likely wiki rounding slip — used as published anyway, flagged below). Permanent uses Spent Essence (lifetime total); Current/Temporary uses Current Essence (what you're holding right now); combined multiplier = (1+Current/100)×(1+Permanent/100).

Validated against a live screenshot (Current Essence back-solved from Mana Refill, Spent Essence = the screenshot's own "Essence Generated" reading, Generator Bonus multiplier left at 1, Trophy count unknown at the time so left at 0): Meditation, Gold, Exp, Dungeon and Gathering all landed within 0.5-5% of the screenshot's real numbers — a good match. Offense, Defense, Reincarnate and Battlefield's Permanent reading ran 5-25% off instead — the Achievement Trophy system (see the Trophy input above) is now confirmed real and gives Offense/Defense DOUBLE the rate of most other types (0.1%/trophy vs 0.05%), which plausibly explains most or all of that gap once you enter your own real Trophy count; Battlefield isn't covered by the Trophy system at all though, so its own ~13% gap is still unexplained. Not modeled: the 6 "GenV2" types (Demons/Conjuring/Generator/Dungeon/Slime/Alchemy V2, unlocked Stage 380-475, shown as a separate "CHARGE AVAILABLE"/"NEXT CHARGE IN" block with its own Spirit-Invested currency) — the wiki doesn't cover these at all and they weren't individually traced from the DLL this pass. Also not modeled: the exact ~7-term generatorBonusBonus chain (Dwarven Workshop level, GenV2 Row 2 permanent bonus, Synergy layer 13, a Pomos-talent ×1.0025^level compounding term, Black Hole level/20, a Challenge-14-only branch, Machinist/Cultist class bonuses) — approximated as a single multiplier above rather than guessing each term's current value. Gold's temporary-bonus coefficient (.667) didn't cleanly match the expected "permanent coefficient ÷ 2" pattern every other type follows (expected .6); used the wiki's literal published value rather than silently "fixing" it.

Reincarnation

Confirmed directly from the in-game Reincarnation screen's own text: "Reincarnation bonus is applied to Mana Refill & Mana Power / Offense & Defense / Meditation / Essence / Dungeon", and "If you reincarnate Spell, Conjuring, Demons, Meditation, Town & Shadow are reset." This is more trustworthy than the wiki's reset-list table, whose column structure (What Resets / What Doesn't) was lost in text extraction and couldn't be cleanly reconstructed — the in-game text above is used as the source of truth for what resets.

Real per-component formula, confirmed from ReincarnationBonus.RBRefresh and reproduced to within 0.06% of a live screenshot's 6 displayed numbers: component% = f(rawStat) × M, where M is a single shared multiplier (ReincBonusBD × SReincBonus × (1+Triangle(perk)×0.1)) and the 4 components are Stage=MaxStage/3, Conjuring=3^(0.25×log10(ManaSpent)+0.8), Meditation=TotalMeditationLevel/3, Demonic Essence=3^(0.25×log10(DELife)+2.3) (0 if you haven't unlocked Darkness). "Total Meditation Level" is a separate system from the Generator's "Meditation" type — it's the sum of your Mana/Stats/Income Meditation levels (3 demon-investment tracks, their own screen) — confirmed by matching 136+191+104=431 against your screenshot's "Demons meditation level: 431" exactly.

M itself is a ~15-20 term chain (confirmed terms include: Achievement Trophy count, the Generator's Dungeon-type current/permanent bonus — see the Generator tab, 2 Talent levels, 2 Synergy layer bonuses, a Challenge-6 completion term, Plaza's Ancient Library level, a Black Hole level, a Cultist class bonus, GenV2 Dungeon permanent, and 4 distinct Cooking terms — Row6Perma/SRow3Perma are permanent, but SRow3Buff and Line17Buff are temporary (Cooking buffs last only 10min per the Cooking screen's own note)) — not decomposed into inputs here, that's a lot of surface for one pass. This is very likely exactly why your screenshot's "Current" (364.2, locked in at your last reincarnation) and "Next" (143.7, live right now) M differed by ~2.5x: a temporary Cooking buff was almost certainly active when Current was locked in and isn't active now — confirmed mechanically real, not guessed. Back-solve M from any one bonus% you can currently see (M = shown% ÷ the component's raw formula above) rather than cook first and re-check if you want an apples-to-apples comparison.

Meditation

Confirmed from the Demons wiki page: Demons assigned to boosts also increase meditation — Mana Refill/Power boosts feed Mana Meditation, Offense/Defense feed Stats Meditation, Exp/Gold feed Income Meditation; demons on "Boost All" split evenly 3 ways. The earlier Boost-counter vs. Meditation-screen mismatch is now fully solved — traced Meditation.Update directly: manaSlaves = (ManaPower + ManaRefill boost demons) + (PermaSlaves + PullPermaSlaves + Mood-bonus demons + BoostAll demons + Event demons + Daily demons) / 3, same shape for Stats/Income. So the Meditation screen's number isn't just your 2 boost counters — it also bakes in a hidden shared pool (global PermaSlaves, fed by certain Conjuring spells like Crappis/Azam through a separate path that never touches the Boost-assignment screen, plus a Pull bonus, a Mood bonus, Event/Daily temporary demons) averaged across all 3 tracks. It's not a bug or a missing system — it's just invisible from the Demons Boost-assignment screen alone. Crafting crystals were checked and ruled out (none of the 3 Exp-per-tick formulas reference Crafting/crystal fields at all, despite several crystal tiers listing "Meditation %" as a stat — that bonus feeds something else, not yet traced). Enter your own Demons-invested count per track directly from your Meditation screen (still the most reliable route — the shared-pool term above isn't exposed as plain save fields either). "Total Meditation Level" (the sum of all 3 levels) is exactly what feeds the Reincarnation tab's Meditation component — confirmed to <0.1% against a live screenshot (136+191+104=431 matched "Demons meditation level: 431" exactly).

Exp-to-level formula confirmed from Meditation.CheckExp (ilsym), each track has its own 2-tier exponent (steeper after a level threshold): Mana=((lvl²+100)^(1.5+lvl/50))^5 (after level 150, the inner exponent also ×(1+(lvl-150)/100)); Stats=(lvl²+100)^(1.5+lvl/80) (after 200, ×(1+(lvl-200)/100)); Income=((lvl²+100)^(1.5+lvl/40))^10 (after 100, ×(1+(lvl-100)/100)). The actual Exp-GAIN-per-tick formula (ManaExpPerTick etc.) is a ~30-term chain — even bigger than Reincarnation's M — pulling in nearly every system in the game (Spell levels, Town's Pagoda, the Generator's own Meditation-type bonus, Achievement Trophy, Reincarnation's own bonus, Cooking, Classes, Synergy, Talents, Plaza Perks, a genuine AlchemyLab/Slime-potion bonus term, and a confirmed Adventure gear term — every piece of Adventure equipment can roll its own hidden "Meditation" stat, equipped gear counts in full and bagged/unequipped gear counts at 10% (+1%/Talent level), all summed into PullMeditation in MinionMain.UpdateStats and applied as a flat ×(1+PullMeditation/100) multiplier identically to all 3 tracks — confirmed this is a genuinely separate feed from Crafting crystals, which don't touch Meditation's formulas at all). Rather than guess at 30 terms, enter your own observed Exp/tick per track (watch the bar for a few ticks, same honest-input pattern as the Gathering/Spell tabs) — PullMeditation isn't saved to your save file either (recomputed live from your full gear loadout each time stats update), so there's no shortcut to auto-filling it without modeling the entire Adventure Items drop table (hundreds of items across 30+ zones) — a real but large undertaking, not done this pass.

Cooking a recipe gives a temporary buff (10min at row level 1, +2:30/row level) AND increases that row's permanent bonus by 2% of its level-1 value, per cook — applied to ALL 3 of that row's stats equally (confirmed from a live screenshot's own note: "if row 1 perma is 3.45%, this is applied to OFF/DEF, Essence & Gold"). Adventure Items — equipment mechanics

Confirmed from the Adventure Items wiki + DLL trace of MinionMain.UpdateStats: every dropped item (Armor/Equipment) can roll ~48 possible stats (Attack/Defense plus named "Bonuses" like Meditation, Gold, Drop Chance, etc), each with a base value and a per-level increase that scales as the item merges up (merging a duplicate raises its level — stats roughly double by max level). Equipped items (one per slot: Head/Shield/Chest/Hands/Legs/Feet/Accessory/Ring/Weapon) count at full value. Unequipped/bagged items still count, at 10% + Talent/Trait bonus (confirmed in code as 0.1 + (ATAdvItems+TSAdvItems)×0.01 — the wiki describes this as "+10% from Arena, +5% from Synergy V2", consistent with the same mechanism). All equipped+bagged items' stats are summed into "Pull" totals (PullMeditation, PullDungeonDamage, PullGoldDrop, etc, ~48 in all) that feed other systems — see the Meditation tab's Demons-invested breakdown for one worked example (Perma Slaves). None of this is saved to your save file — it's recomputed live from your current loadout every time, so there's no way to auto-fill it; you'd read it directly from your own Equipment screen.

Item data below (393 items across 40 zones/sets, transcribed from the wiki) is a browsable reference, not a full optimizer — comparing the true "best" item per slot would need weighing all ~48 stats against everything you're simultaneously training. But the in-game inventory screen itself exposes a goal-oriented "Sort By" list (Meditation, Mana Power, Gather Speed, Dungeon Dmg, etc — the devs' own single-stat shortcut), so the lookup below includes a matching "Rank by goal" dropdown: pick what you're currently focused on (grinding Meditation for Demons, gearing for Dungeon pushes, spell/mana investment, gathering...) and the table re-sorts by each item's BASE value in that stat (level-1 roll, before any merging) so you can scan for the strongest options. It's single-stat ranking, not a true multi-objective score — an item strong in your chosen stat may be weak everywhere else.

Crafting — mechanics (corrects last session's note: crystals ARE equippable gear, not a separate system)

Confirmed from the DLL (Crafting class + ArmorType enum + CharacterPanel's dedicated crystal slots): crafting a crystal (CraftItemVoid/Astral/Divine/Eldrich/Celestial/Abyssal/Cosmic) consumes Gathering resources (Logs for Void/Astral/Divine, Ores for the 3 hybrid types, Gems for Cosmic) and calls AddItem() — the SAME inventory call Adventure gear drops use. Crystals are genuine Armor-class items (ArmorType.Void/Astral/.../Cosmic) equipped into 7 dedicated character-panel slots (plus a Mount slot), so they flow through the identical MinionMain.UpdateStats Pull-aggregation as Adventure gear: equipped = full value, unequipped/bagged = ~10% + Talent/Trait bonus. Each successful craft also has a separate RNG chance (confirmed formula, inversely related to your current skill) to raise your Void/Astral/Divine "Crafting Skill" — that's what unlocks higher crystal tiers, tracked independently from the crystal items themselves.

3 "pure" crystal types (Void→Respawn/Speed/Crit/Double chance line, Astral→Meditation line, Divine→Mana Refill line) plus 4 hybrid types combining two or three pure types' bonuses at smaller per-level rates (Eldrich=Void+Astral, Celestial=Astral+Divine, Abyssal=Void+Divine, Cosmic=all three). Each type has 4 craftable tiers (base/High/Superior/Master), each tier has several "+N" slots that merge like Adventure gear duplicates — merging raises the slot's level up to its max (250 up to 1000 depending on tier). The reference below lists all 89 craftable slots with their cost, unlock requirement, and exact base/per-level effect(s), transcribed from the wiki. A few high-tier "+1" slots have wiki gaps (shown as "?") where the per-level value for some stats wasn't listed — left blank rather than guessed.

Minion Lab

Confirmed from the MinionLab class (its own 7-track feed-upgrade system, unlocked separately from Minion/Minion Adventure — your screenshot confirms it has its own sidebar tab). You feed each track Pet Food: 10 food per feed, each feed adds +1 Level (before bonuses) and starts a 50-tick "digestion" timer (ticks down 2/update) before the next feed is accepted, unless Auto-feed is toggled on for that track. Two Trophy Shop items modify the feed rate (MinionLabLevel1Bonus/MinionLabLevel2Bonus, additive to the +1/feed) and two raise the level cap (MinionLabCap1Bonus +15,000,000, MinionLabCap2Bonus +35,000,000, on top of the base 50,000,000 cap) — enter those if bought on the Trophy Shop tab.

Attack and Defense feed directly into the master Total Attack/Defense formula (as ML.AttackBonus/ML.DefenseBonus — see the calculator on the Stage Roadmap tab). CritDmg/Speed/Drop/Rarity/Respawn/AdvExp feed Critical Damage, Attack Speed, Drop Chance and gathering-adjacent stats elsewhere — shown here for completeness but not part of the Attack/Defense total. Speed and Respawn switch to a different (log-base-20) curve past Level 100,000, confirmed from the DLL — shown in the table once you're past that level.

Challenges

Confirmed from the AcceptChallenge class: 14 repeatable regular Challenges (First Step, Inferno/"Keep Going", Real Estate, and 11 more unnamed in the DLL's own field names — goal values below are from the DLL's exact per-completion formulas) plus 6 one-time "Infinite Challenges" unlocked much later. Every regular Challenge completion — any of the 14, confirmed directly in AcceptChallenge.Update — adds a flat +2.0 to ChallengeAdvBonus, permanently, with no cap seen in the DLL. This is the exact value used in the master Attack/Defense formula as (1 + ChallengeAdvBonus/100) — so every single Challenge completion across all 14 challenges is worth +2% Attack and +2% Defense, forever. This directly matches the stage roadmap's "Challenge completed for rewards" framing: these aren't one-off unlocks, they're a repeatable, uncapped combat-power grind.

Stage Roadmap (the throughline: everything else feeds into clearing the next stage)

Per the wiki's own framing: clearing stages is what unlocks new features, zones and story in Adventure mode — Spells, Town, Demons, Conjuring, Crafting, Generator, Classes, Trophy tiers and Adventure gear all ultimately exist to push your Offense/Defense past the next stage's monster stats (or, late-game, to clear a one-time Challenge gated behind a stage). This tab is the roadmap: every milestone stage from the wiki (77 of them, Stage 4 through 800) with what it unlocks and the Offense/Defense you need to clear it. Two of these milestones are especially load-bearing — Stage 415's "LTWA" Challenge is what first unlocks Synergy, and Stage 670's "Hardest Challenge Ever" unlocks Synergy V2 — both major systems gated behind a one-time Challenge completion, not just raw stage-clearing power.

Offense/Defense numbers are the wiki's own listed monster stats at 0 Temples bought (Temple is a Town building that reduces effective monster power); the wiki stops listing them past Stage ~425 since by then they depend heavily on individual build choices.

Where to put your mana: Spell targeting

This is the in-game SPELL menu (Magic Missile, Fire Bolt...) — a completely different mechanic from Conjuring. You don't buy levels directly: you park mana in a spell, and Mana invested × Mana Power = Exp/sec, which fills an exp bar and levels the spell automatically. Each level adds a flat amount to one stat (Offense for the left/orange column, Defense for the right/cyan column), multiplied by an account-wide bonus multiplier. The 11 left-column spells and 11 right-column spells are paired tier-for-tier (e.g. Magic Missile ↔ Blur) — each pair shares the exact same per-level bonus, exp cost, and mana-investment tier, just applied to Offense vs Defense.

Two things cap how fast a spell can level: a hard 50-levels/sec ceiling, and the exp a level actually costs. The 50/sec ceiling was confirmed by timing Magic Missile and Firewall's level counts across two videos ~5 hours apart (49.97–49.98/sec each, independently). The exp cost itself is level × a fixed per-tier constant — confirmed to an exact round number for 9 of the 11 tiers by dividing each tooltip's "exp needed" figure by its level. Combined with your Mana Power stat, that gives the actual levels/sec each spell is getting right now, and exactly how much mana it'd take to reach the 50/sec ceiling — shown per spell below instead of a manual on/off guess.
