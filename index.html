Generator (Power/Mana)

Current essence is one shared pool for all 15 types, but permanent essence is per generator (each charge release moves essence into one generator; save field generatorsEssence) (confirmed from a live screenshot: Mana Refill and Mana Power read identical values because they use the identical formula on the identical essence pool — this isn't a coincidence, the game really does share one pool across every type). Charges (max 3 base, +1 every 6h, Soul Shop can add more/speed it up) aren't modeled as a timer here — enter your current Spent/Current Essence directly. Formula per type: bonus% = coef × 3^(const + rate × log_base(essence)), confirmed structurally from the game's own code (3^(…) growth curve, per-type coefficient table) and cross-checked against the wiki's published formula table, which matched exactly for 10 of 11 "simple" types (Gold's published current-coefficient looked like a likely wiki rounding slip — used as published anyway, flagged below). Permanent uses Spent Essence (lifetime total); Current/Temporary uses Current Essence (what you're holding right now); combined multiplier = (1+Current/100)×(1+Permanent/100).

Validated against a live screenshot (Current Essence back-solved from Mana Refill, Spent Essence = the screenshot's own "Essence Generated" reading, Generator Bonus multiplier left at 1, Trophy count unknown at the time so left at 0): Meditation, Gold, Exp, Dungeon and Gathering all landed within 0.5-5% of the screenshot's real numbers — a good match. Adventure Items — equipment mechanics

Confirmed from the Adventure Items wiki + DLL trace of MinionMain.UpdateStats: every dropped item (Armor/Equipment) can roll ~48 possible stats (Attack/Defense plus named "Bonuses" like Meditation, Gold, Drop Chance, etc), each with a base value and a per-level increase that scales as the item merges up (merging a duplicate raises its level — stats roughly double by max level). Equipped items (one per slot: Head/Shield/Chest/Hands/Legs/Feet/Accessory/Ring/Weapon) count at full value. Unequipped/bagged items still count, at 10% + Talent/Trait bonus (confirmed in code as 0.1 + (ATAdvItems+TSAdvItems)×0.01 — the wiki describes this as "+10% from Arena, +5% from Synergy V2", consistent with the same mechanism). All equipped+bagged items' stats are summed into "Pull" totals (PullMeditation, PullDungeonDamage, PullGoldDrop, etc, ~48 in all) that feed other systems — see the Meditation tab's Demons-invested breakdown for one worked example (Perma Slaves). None of this is saved to your save file — it's recomputed live from your current loadout every time, so there's no way to auto-fill it; you'd read it directly from your own Equipment screen.

Plaza ratio planner

For auto-prestige. Each prestige rebuilds tiers 1–5 to your ratio (each tier needs both buildings of the tier below at 5, and tier 5 needs 2 to prestige). Every building's cost is taken from each of the three gathering tracks, and its mana cost comes out of next-life mana first; whatever next-life mana can't cover is taken from your mana bar and permanently burned off your mana cap (PlazaTown.Click*Upgrade). Auto-build never waits for next-life mana: if the bar holds enough, it burns cap. Next-life mana grows by mana created ÷ the "+1 per …" number, and that number scales with your cap, so a smaller cap refills next-life mana faster. That's why the cap settles at the level where next-life income equals Plaza spending: with auto on, the cap only shrinks, and a smaller cap means less mana per refill can go into conjuring and spells. The goal is an endless engine: a ratio whose cost per hour fits inside what you gather and what next-life mana grows back, so it never starts and stalls. This planner compares income and spending per hour, names the bottleneck, and searches every legal ratio for the most perks per hour that still fits. Where to put your mana: Spell targeting

This is the in-game SPELL menu (Magic Missile, Fire Bolt...) — a completely different mechanic from Conjuring. You don't buy levels directly: you park mana in a spell, and Mana invested × Mana Power = Exp/sec, which fills an exp bar and levels the spell automatically. Each level adds a flat amount to one stat (Offense for the left/orange column, Defense for the right/cyan column), multiplied by an account-wide bonus multiplier. The 11 left-column spells and 11 right-column spells are paired tier-for-tier (e.g. Magic Missile ↔ Blur) — each pair shares the exact same per-level bonus, exp cost, and mana-investment tier, just applied to Offense vs Defense.

Two things cap how fast a spell can level: a hard 50-levels/sec ceiling, and the exp a level actually costs. The 50/sec ceiling was confirmed by timing Magic Missile and Firewall's level counts across two videos ~5 hours apart (49.97–49.98/sec each, independently). The exp cost itself is level × a fixed per-tier constant — confirmed to an exact round number for 9 of the 11 tiers by dividing each tooltip's "exp needed" figure by its level. Combined with your Mana Power stat, that gives the actual levels/sec each spell is getting right now, and exactly how much mana it'd take to reach the 50/sec ceiling — shown per spell below instead of a manual on/off guess.
